Faculties

Faculty of Science and Technology

The Faculty of Science and Technology (FST) at Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is dedicated to providing high-quality education and research opportunities in various scientific disciplines. The faculty offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in fields such as marine science, environmental science, biotechnology, and information technology. FST is committed to fostering innovation, critical thinking, and practical skills among its students, preparing them for successful careers in academia, industry, and research.

Faculty of Maritime Studies and Marine Science

The Faculty of Maritime Studies and Marine Science (FMSMS) at Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) focuses on the study of marine and maritime sciences, including oceanography, marine biology, fisheries science, and maritime engineering. The faculty aims to produce graduates who are well-equipped with the knowledge and skills needed to address the challenges facing the marine environment and maritime industries. FMSMS emphasizes hands-on learning experiences, research opportunities, and collaboration with industry partners to ensure that students gain practical insights into their chosen fields.

Faculty of Ocean Engineering and Technology

The Faculty of Ocean Engineering and Technology (FOET) at Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is dedicated to advancing knowledge and skills in ocean engineering and related technologies. The faculty offers programs in areas such as offshore engineering, coastal engineering, and marine renewable energy. FOET is committed to fostering innovation and research excellence, preparing students for careers in the rapidly evolving field of ocean engineering.

Faculty of Fisheries and Food Science

The Faculty of Fisheries and Food Science (FFFS) at Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) focuses on the study of fisheries, aquaculture, and food science. The faculty offers programs that cover various aspects of fisheries management, seafood processing, and food safety. FFFS aims to produce graduates who are knowledgeable in sustainable fisheries practices, aquaculture techniques, and food quality assurance. The faculty emphasizes research and practical training to equip students with the skills needed to contribute to the development of the fisheries and food industries.

Faculty of Science and Marine Environment

The Faculty of Science and Marine Environment (FSME) at Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is dedicated to the study of marine ecosystems, environmental science, and conservation. The faculty offers programs that focus on understanding the complex interactions between marine organisms and their environments. FSME aims to produce graduates who are equipped with the knowledge and skills needed to address environmental challenges, promote sustainable practices, and contribute to the preservation of marine biodiversity. The faculty emphasizes research, fieldwork, and community engagement to foster a deeper understanding of marine environments.

Faculty of Business, Economics & Social Development (FPEPS)

The Faculty of Business, Economics & Social Development (FPEPS) at Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is committed to providing quality education and research opportunities in the fields of business, economics, and social development. The faculty offers programs that prepare students for careers in various sectors, including finance, marketing, public policy, and social services. FPEPS emphasizes practical learning, critical thinking, and ethical responsibility to produce graduates who can contribute to the economic and social advancement of the region and the nation.

Faculty of Food Science and Agrotechnology (FSMA)

The Faculty of Food Science and Agrotechnology (FSMA) at Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) focuses on the study of food science, agricultural technology, and related disciplines. The faculty offers programs that cover various aspects of food production, processing, and safety. FSMA aims to produce graduates who are knowledgeable in modern food technologies, agricultural practices, and sustainable development. The faculty emphasizes research and practical training to equip students with the skills needed to contribute to the advancement of the food and agriculture industries.

History

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) telah bermula sebagai Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) dengan sebuah Pusat Perikanan dan Sains Samudera pada tahun 1979, yang menyediakan kemudahan latihan pelajar Program Perikanan dan Sains Samudera di samping menyediakan kemudahan penyelidikan untuk pensyarah.

Melalui Penstrukturan semula program akademik di UPM, keseluruhan Fakulti Perikanan dan Sains Samudera telah dipindahkan ke Terengganu dan diberi nama baru iaitu Fakulti Sains Gunaan dan Teknologi (FSGT) mulai Jun 1996. Turut ditubuhkan ialah Fakulti Sains dan Sastera Ikhtisas (FSSI) dan Pusat Pengajian Matrikulasi (PPM).

Mulai Jun 1996, kampus ini telah diiktiraf (secara dalaman UPM) sebagai sebuah pusat tanggungjawab dan dinamakan Universiti Putra Malaysia Cawangan Terengganu (UPMT) dan diketuai oleh seorang Rektor (designate).

Achievements

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) has achieved significant milestones in various fields of education, research, and community engagement. The university has been recognized for its contributions to marine science, fisheries, and environmental sustainability. UMT has established partnerships with international institutions, enhancing its research capabilities and providing students with global exposure. The university's commitment to innovation and excellence has led to numerous awards and accolades, solidifying its reputation as a leading institution in Malaysia.
